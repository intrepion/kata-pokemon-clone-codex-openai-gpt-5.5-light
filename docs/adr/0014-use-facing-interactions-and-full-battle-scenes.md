# Use facing interactions and full battle scenes

Important NPCs will be blocking tile characters interacted with through Facing Interaction, and encounters will transition into a full Battle Scene rather than appearing as an overlay panel. This keeps the world tactile and preserves the modal shift that makes classic creature RPG battles feel distinct from exploration.

**Consequences**

Professor Grove and Badge Meadow should use character placement and facing direction as part of their interaction contract, while signs can remain secondary static prompts.
