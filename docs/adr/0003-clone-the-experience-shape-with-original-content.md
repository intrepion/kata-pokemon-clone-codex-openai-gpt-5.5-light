# Clone the experience shape with original content

We will clone the experience shape of early Pokemon without using official Pokemon names, art, creatures, regions, music, or protected terminology in the game world. The project should feel recognizable through its top-down exploration, wild encounters, party battles, capture loop, field guide, and badge progression, not through copied assets or names.

**Consequences**

Canonical domain terms use Creature, Field Guide, Route, Trainer Battle, Capture, and Badge rather than official franchise labels.
