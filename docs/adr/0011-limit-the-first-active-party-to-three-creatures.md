# Limit the first active party to three creatures

The First Badge Loop will use an Active Party Limit of three creatures instead of the iconic six. Three creatures are enough to teach starter choice, capture, party switching, and preparation for Badge Meadow without forcing a larger party UI and balance surface into the first playable arc.

**Consequences**

The first implementation may still track seen and captured creatures in the Field Guide, but battle switching and active roster UI should optimize for three carried creatures.
